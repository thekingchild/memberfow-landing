The problem

Your membership grew.
Your tools didn't.

New applications arrive by email. Dues live in a spreadsheet. Renewal dates sit in one officer's head. Every register is a different file, and no two ever agree.

  • Applications scattered across inboxes, paper forms, and WhatsApp messages
  • Payment records that don't reconcile with what members actually paid
  • Expired memberships discovered only at the worst moment
  • The same member recorded three different ways in three different files

Membersflow replaces that patchwork with one system of record — from first application to every renewal after it.

What Membersflow can do

Everything your register needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

One portal covering the full membership lifecycle: apply, approve, pay, renew, and stay found.

Applications and approvals

Publish your membership tracks and receive applications online, complete with application fees. Review each one as a team, approve as requested, or move an applicant to a different track with a recorded reason. Every decision is written down and traceable.

Dues and payments that reconcile

Collect dues through Paystack or Flutterwave, or accept bank transfers with uploaded payment proof your finance officer verifies. Every payment produces an immutable invoice and receipt, so your records never drift from reality.

Renewals without the chase

Membership terms and expiry dates are tracked for you. Members get reminders before they lapse, and your register always shows who is current, due, or expired — no spreadsheet archaeology required.

Membership IDs and cards

Issue unique membership numbers using your own serial or randomized scheme, and give every member a printable and PDF membership card with QR validity they can present anywhere.

A member area people actually use

Members manage their own profile and photo, track applications, view invoices and payment history, download their card and receipts, and finish unfinished applications — without calling your office.

A public directory you control

Publish an opt-in, search-first directory of your members, organized around your own classes, chapters, divisions, and specialties. Members choose what appears; private details never enter search results.

News that reaches the right audience

Publish articles, notices, and announcements to the public, to members only, or to specific membership types and levels — so the right message lands with the right people.

Bring your existing register with you

Import current members through a guided CSV upload with validation and a dry-run preview, so nothing lands in the register wrong. Historical records stay intact and attributable.

Payments

Payments the way your members actually pay

Cards & online

Card payments, confirmed automatically

Members pay their application fees and dues by card through Paystack or Flutterwave. Payments are confirmed automatically, and every transaction produces its own invoice and receipt.

Bank transfers

Bank transfers, verified properly

Some members will always prefer the bank. Membersflow publishes your current account instructions, accepts uploaded payment proof, and routes it to your finance officer for review and settlement against the right invoice. Stale account details become a thing of the past.

Records

Records that hold up

Invoices, receipts, and payment records are immutable once issued. When a member asks "didn't I pay last year?", the answer is one lookup away — with the amount, the date, and who verified it.
