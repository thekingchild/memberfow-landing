Already built

Everything your register needs. Nothing it doesn't.

One portal covering the full membership lifecycle: apply, approve, pay, renew, and stay found. Every item below is working software you can walk through on a demo.

  1. Applications and approvals

    Publish your membership tracks and receive applications online, with application fees if you charge them. Review each one as a team, approve as requested, or move an applicant to a different track with a recorded reason.

  2. Dues and payments that reconcile

    Collect through Paystack or Flutterwave, or accept bank transfers with uploaded proof that your finance officer verifies. Every payment produces an immutable invoice and receipt.

  3. Renewals without the chase

    Terms and expiry dates are tracked for you. Members get reminders before they lapse, and the register always shows who is current, due, or expired.

  4. Membership IDs and cards

    Issue membership numbers using your own serial or randomized scheme, and give every member a printable PDF card with a QR code that checks their standing live.

  5. A member area people actually use

    Members manage their profile and photo, track applications, view invoices and payment history, download their card and receipts, and finish unfinished applications without calling your office.

  6. A public directory you control

    An opt-in, search-first directory organized around your own classes, chapters, divisions and specialties. Members choose what appears; private details never enter search results.

  7. News that reaches the right audience

    Publish articles and announcements to the public, to members only, or to specific membership types and levels.

  8. Bring your existing register with you

    A guided CSV import validates every row, up to 5,000 at a time, and shows a dry-run preview before anything is applied. Historical records stay intact and attributable.

Payments

Payments the way your members actually pay.

Card payments through Paystack or Flutterwave are confirmed automatically. Bank transfers are accepted with uploaded proof and settled by your finance officer against the right invoice. Either way, the record holds up.

Card payment, confirmed by webhook.

The member pays from their invoice. Paystack confirms the transaction to the portal directly, the invoice flips to paid, and a receipt is issued. Nobody re-keys anything.

  • Verified end to end in sandbox, with webhooks
  • Amount checked against the invoice before it is marked paid
  • Receipt available in the member area immediately

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, write to hello@membersflow.ng.

Do we need our own servers or an IT team?

No. Membersflow is fully managed. We deploy, host and maintain the portal for you. Your staff and members only need a browser.

How do we get started?

Book a demo. We walk you through the portal, then configure your membership structure, branding and payment setup, and import your existing register as part of onboarding.

Which payment methods are supported?

Paystack and Flutterwave for online card payments, plus bank transfer with uploaded proof that your finance team verifies and settles. Manual payments can also be recorded.

Does it charge members' cards automatically?

No. Renewal is manual by design: members receive reminders and renew themselves. There are no surprise debits.

Can we import our existing members?

Yes. A guided CSV import validates every row and shows a dry-run preview before anything is applied to your register.

Is the public directory compulsory?

No. Publication is opt-in per member and configurable by the organization. Many bodies run entirely without a public directory.

Will it match how our organization is structured?

Yes. Membership classes and grades, chapters and regions, divisions and specialties are all configurable. The system adopts your vocabulary.
